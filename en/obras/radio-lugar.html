Rádio Lugar

2010 · urban intervention

Conexão Artes Visuais, Funarte — Projeto EME>>, 2010

Sound recording in preparation.

Intervention with MP3 players and loudspeakers sewn onto ordinary shoes, worn by dancers on the Rio–Niterói ferry crossing and at Praça XV. Projeto EME>>, Conexão Artes Visuais, Funarte, 2010.
