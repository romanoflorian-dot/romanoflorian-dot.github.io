Turbina

2013 / 2023 · installation

Use headphones

Wall-shaped installation of PVC pipes and painted wood that plays recordings of the sea. Shown in the exhibition Sonar, Casa de Cultura Laura Alvim (2013), and in A Pele do Mundo, Futuros – Arte e Tecnologia (2023).

Materials
Wood, acrylic paint, PVC, recording of the sea
Dimensions
261 × 263 cm (2013); 410 × 285 × 30 cm (2023)
Venue
Casa de Cultura Laura Alvim (2013); Futuros – Arte e Tecnologia (2023)
Collection
Sérgio Carvalho Collection
Exhibition
A Pele do Mundo (2023)
