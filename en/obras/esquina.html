Esquina

2023 · installation

Sound recording in preparation.

A corner is where paths cross and sound changes direction. Sensors set off engines and traffic: whoever walks composes the city they hear.

Materials
Wood, acrylic paint, audio, presence sensors
Dimensions
Variable dimensions
Venue
Futuros – Arte e Tecnologia, Rio de Janeiro
Exhibition
A Pele do Mundo (2023)
