Chuveiros Sonoros

2008 · sound object

Use headphones

The tap opens a voice, not water. Whoever steps in lends their body to an anonymous singer.

Materials
Stainless steel showers, amplifier, loudspeaker and home recordings
Dimensions
249 × 70 × 70 cm
Venue
7th Mercosul Biennial, Mostra Absurdo, Porto Alegre (2009); Casa de Cultura Laura Alvim (2013)
Collection
Museu de Arte Contemporânea de Niterói — acquired through the Marcantonio Vilaça Award, Funarte, 2012
Exhibition
Sonar (2013), A Pele do Mundo (2023)
