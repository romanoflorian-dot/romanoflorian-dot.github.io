Muro de Som

2016 · installation

Use headphones

Two walls of loudspeakers blow a wind made of the artist's own breath. In the ruins, the wall does not divide: it opens a way through.

Materials
Two walls with 32 loudspeakers each, sound of wind
Dimensions
2 walls, 330 × 170 cm
Venue
Parque das Ruínas, Rio de Janeiro
Exhibition
Muro de Som (2016)
