Errância

2016 · installation

CCBB Contemporary Award, 2015

Sound recording in preparation.

Phrases overheard in the city's bars, recorded by a drifting body. Without an origin, the fragments turn speech into common noise.

Award
CCBB Contemporary Award, 2015
Materials
Loudspeakers; recordings made with a capture system worn by a passer-by
Dimensions
Variable dimensions
Venue
Sala A Contemporânea, Centro Cultural Banco do Brasil, Rio de Janeiro
Exhibition
Errância (2016)
